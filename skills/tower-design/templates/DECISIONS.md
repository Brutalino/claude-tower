# Decisions

A dated log of decisions taken with the owner. Newest at the bottom. A
decision is never edited in place: when it changes, a new entry records the
override and points at the old one. Before acting on a request, check it
against this list.

Format: date, decision, the options weighed with their trade-offs, why this
one, what it rules out; the evidence (a research finding, a prototype in
`spikes/`) when there is one.

## <YYYY-MM> (<stage or theme, e.g. "design: platform, stack, data">)

- **<YYYY-MM-DD> — <The decision, in one sentence.>** Options: <A> (<its
  trade-off>), <B> (<its trade-off>), <C> (<its trade-off>). Chosen: <A>,
  because <the reason, in the owner's words when they gave it>. Rules out
  <what can no longer be proposed>. Evidence: <RESEARCH.md, section; or
  `spikes/<nn>-<slug>/SPIKE.md`>.
- **<YYYY-MM-DD> — <Stage 6, design, skipped>.** <The product has no UI: …>.
